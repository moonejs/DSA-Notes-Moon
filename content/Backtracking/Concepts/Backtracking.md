**Backtracking** is a **problem-solving technique** where:

> We **try one possible choice**,  
> if it **doesn’t work**, we **go back (backtrack)** and try **another choice**.

![[Pasted image 20260107111845.jpg]]


### Types of Backtracking

1. Decision-Based Backtracking (kya starting point se end point jane ka rasta hai ? YES or NO)
2. Optimization-Based Backtracking(sabse chota rasta konsa hai jane ka)
3. Enumeration-Based (**Listing ALL possible solutions** of a problem **one by one**.)(sare raste bta do jane ke )
