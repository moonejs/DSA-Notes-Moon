### What are Vertices?

Vertices are the **objects**.

Examples:

- People
    
- Cities
    
- Courses
    
- Intersections
    
- Students
    

Think of vertices as **dots**.

### What are Edges?

Edges represent **relationships between vertices**.

Think of edges as **lines connecting dots**.

Examples:

- A is friend of B
    
- City A has a flight to City B
    
- Teacher teaches Course
    
- Two students are friends
